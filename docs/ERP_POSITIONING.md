# TGG Ops × Trimble ERP — Positioning One-Pager

*Talk track for the demo. TG Gallagher went live on a Trimble ERP
(Viewpoint) earlier this year — this is how TGG Ops fits alongside it.*

---

## The one-liner

> "Your ERP is the system of record for money. TGG Ops is the system of
> record for **what actually happens in the field and the fab shop** — and
> it turns that into estimating intelligence and service revenue your ERP
> can't see."

## What we are NOT

Not a replacement for Vista/Spectrum. GL, AP/AR, job costing, union
payroll, certified payroll, subcontracts, WIP, revenue recognition — that
stays exactly where it is. You just invested in that go-live; this builds
on it.

## The three gaps TGG Ops fills

**1. Field operations at reading-level granularity.**
A tech standing at AHU-3 enters a temperature split on a phone. That
becomes a color-coded reading, a finding with an NFPA/ASHRAE code
reference, and a customer-ready PDF the same day. ERP service modules
invoice the visit; they don't capture the 19°F → 11°F drift that predicts
the failure.
*Demo: open a work order on the phone → readings → AI summary → PDF.*

**2. The prefab feedback loop.**
The ERP knows labor dollars by cost code. TGG Ops knows a mech rack is
estimated at 50 shop hours and actually takes 59 (+18%), while HVAC spools
run 7% under. That per-assembly-type variance goes straight back into the
next estimate — and it's the dataset BIM/VDC is uniquely positioned to own.
*Demo: Prefab pipeline → Insights est-vs-actual chart.*

**3. The AI layer.**
Executive summaries drafted from readings and findings; failure trends
surfaced from service history. Trimble isn't shipping this today — and the
contractor who accumulates this data first has a moat.
*Demo: Insights page — the AHU-3 drift story.*

## The integration story (when they ask)

- **Phase 1 (now):** standalone; adopt ERP job numbers and cost codes so
  every record speaks the ERP's language.
- **Phase 2:** push completed work orders + shop hours into Vista job
  costing — ERP gets *more* accurate, not bypassed.
- **Phase 3:** pull contract/customer master data from the ERP so there's
  a single source of truth for both.

## Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We just went live on Trimble." | "Perfect timing — this makes that investment smarter. We sync to it, not around it." |
| "Doesn't Trimble do field service?" | "It invoices service. It doesn't do phone-first readings, same-day compliance PDFs, or learn from the data." |
